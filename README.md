# Horizons Unbound

The bells start ringing before you can see the storm. By the time its rings rise above the trees, the sky has already changed colour.

Horizons Unbound brings four kinds of dimensional storm into Minecraft. Each carries a little of another world: its weather, its creatures, its ground, and a few things you would rather have left on the other side.

[![The birth of the Void Rift](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/dcvke/horizons-unbound-media/main/posters/void-rift-birth.jpg)](https://dcvke.github.io/horizons-unbound-media/video/void-rift-birth.mp4)

*Click to watch the Void Rift arrive.*

## Out on the horizon

Storms announce themselves, rise, and drift across the landscape before falling apart. A storm lasts fifteen minutes by default. You can follow it, take shelter, or try to reach its centre while you still have time.

Along the way, familiar ground can give way to patches of another dimension. Pieces of structures appear beneath the column. Creatures arrive with it. Sometimes two storms meet, and their effects come together.

Births, first encounters and endings have their own cutscenes, with your character taking part. Hold **Shift** to skip, or turn cutscenes off in your settings.

## Know the colours

![Storms and their effects](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/2a5ef9c5cf2462901bd4f60abb1abf1be91f260a_0.webp)

### Void Rift · End

Purple rings, hanging arrows and distances that no longer feel quite right. Gravity shifts beneath the column; seams open in the air, and you may catch sight of an echo wearing your skin. A **Reality Anchor** gives you a small patch of steady ground.

![The purple Void Rift](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/dcvke/horizons-unbound-media/main/webp-compact/end-storm.webp)

### Fire Twister · Nether

The column brightens. A wave of heat rolls out, leaving Hot Crust underfoot and ash in the air. There is something to gain from getting close: equipment dropped in its heart can be tempered by a passing heat wave.

![The orange Fire Twister](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/dcvke/horizons-unbound-media/main/webp-compact/nether-storm.webp)

### Thunder Funnel · Overworld

Snow gathers on one side of the storm while new streams run through the other. Watch for glowing ground before lightning strikes. If you make it into the eye, take a moment to enjoy the quiet.

![The blue Thunder Funnel](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/dcvke/horizons-unbound-media/main/webp-compact/overworld-storm.webp)

### Deep Whisper · Deep Dark

Sounds fade inside the teal column. Your footsteps still count. The Deep Whisper follows vibrations and answers repeated noise with Darkness. With Simple Voice Chat installed, it hears your voice too.

![The teal Deep Whisper](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/dcvke/horizons-unbound-media/main/webp-compact/deep-dark-storm.webp)

## Before you play

Install Horizons Unbound on **the server and every player's client**. For singleplayer, install it in your own game. Choose the file for your Minecraft version and loader.

| Minecraft | Loaders | Java | Required on Fabric |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1.21.1 | NeoForge 21.1.265+ or Fabric Loader 0.20.1+ | 21 | Fabric API 0.116.18+ and Forge Config API Port 21.1.6+ |
| 1.20.1 | Forge 47.4.29+ or Fabric Loader 0.20.1+ | 17 | Fabric API 0.92.0+ and Forge Config API Port 8.0.3+ |

Use dependencies made for the same Minecraft version. Mod Menu is optional on Fabric if you want to open the settings from the mods menu. NeoForge and Forge builds need no additional mods.

### Your world, your weather

Change how often storms appear, how long they last, their size and speed, the creatures they bring, and whether they change the terrain. Open **Mods → Horizons Unbound → Config** to get started. Each player has their own volume and cutscene settings.

The mod follows Minecraft's **Hide Lightning Flashes** and **Distortion Effects** settings. Storms and advancements also work with cutscenes disabled.

### Playing with other mods

There is support for Iris/Oculus shader setups, Distant Horizons, JourneyMap, Xaero's maps, Figura and Simple Voice Chat. If a shader makes a storm look wrong, try changing **Shader pack mode** in the client settings. More help is in the [compatibility guide](docs/wiki/Server-and-Compatibility.md).

### What about your builds?

Storms protect recorded player-placed blocks, areas around world spawn and beds, and supported land claims. Buildings made before the mod was installed may need extra protection; claim support depends on the claim mod. Read [the protection notes](docs/wiki/Server-and-Compatibility.md) before adding storms to an older world.
