# Thunder Funnel

> Snow on one bank. Rain on the other. The river between them had not been there in the morning.

The blue storm is easy to underestimate. You already know what thunder sounds like, and you have walked home in worse rain. Then the ground starts behaving like several seasons have arrived together.

## Reading the weather

Overworld effects include weather bands, frost and thaw, rain pockets, streams and charged ground. A glowing patch can warn of an approaching lightning strike. Watch the ground as well as the cloud: the warning is useful precisely because the sky is already full of distractions.

The column has a calmer eye. Reaching that space is part of the encounter, and standing within a column is associated with the `eye` advancement. A calm centre should not be confused with a guarantee that all nearby terrain, creatures or effects have stopped mattering.

Ground changes and water features still operate within the mod's terrain and protection rules. The storm is not permission to overwrite every block beneath it. Existing builds, spawn and beds, protected tags and claim hooks are discussed in [server notes](Server-and-Compatibility.md).

## What comes with it

Skeletons and zombies are in the default creature list for an Overworld storm. The mod's creature subsystem is separate from vanilla weather spawning, has a default cap of four creatures per storm and does not spawn its creatures in Peaceful.

When configured to occur in other dimensions, an Overworld storm can bring its own terrain and structure fragments there. Natural spawning outside the Overworld is configurable but less tested; an operator demonstration is not evidence that every modded dimension has been verified.

For a controlled look at the blue storm, `/storm omen overworld 150` includes the announced arrival. `/storm spawn overworld 150` places one immediately. Both require operator permission level 2.
