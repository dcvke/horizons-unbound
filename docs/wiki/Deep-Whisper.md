# Deep Whisper

> I stopped walking and the light stopped following me. Then I told myself that was a coincidence. It heard that too.

The teal storm makes the landscape sound farther away. The temptation is to hurry through the quiet. That is often the first thing it notices.

## What it hears

Inside the column, the storm reacts to player-caused vibration events: footsteps, jumps, hits, and placing or breaking blocks can matter. Sneaking suppresses the vibration events Minecraft marks as ignorable while sneaking. It is not a blanket exemption for everything a crouching player can do.

Visible pulses trace a heard noise towards the column. The storm also listens to voice activity when the optional Simple Voice Chat integration is installed. Voice chat is not required to play the mod.

In the checked 1.21.1 implementation, four counted noises within a ten-second window trigger four seconds of **Darkness**. The listener limits repeated pulses to one per half-second. Darkness is the response; this listening effect does not directly deal damage.

## Creatures and expectations

The default Deep Dark creature list is **empty**. The storm does not automatically summon a Warden just because it can hear you. A server owner may deliberately add one through `deepDarkMobs`, or choose other entity IDs. Check the server's configuration before treating a video from another modpack as the default behaviour.

The same separation applies to scenery. Deep Dark terrain and ancient-city fragment candidates belong to the dimension-pieces system, which can be adjusted or disabled independently of the listening effect.

## Moving through it

Slow down. Give the pulses time to tell you what the storm registered. Repeated jumps, breaking a shortcut through the ground, or talking your way through a voice-chat session can make the encounter feel very different from a quiet approach.

Turning down your speaker volume changes what you hear; it does not make your character's vibration events disappear. Personal sound settings and gameplay detection serve different purposes.

The journal never settles whether the storm is listening to the traveller or waiting for something beyond them to answer. You can leave that question open while still learning which actions it notices.
