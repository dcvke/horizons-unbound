# Fire Twister

> I laid the pickaxe down because the handle was burning my hand. When I came back, the iron had a colour I had only seen inside a furnace.

An orange column turns the surrounding landscape into a moving forge. Its quiet moments matter: the storm charges, sends a wave outward, and leaves you a little time to decide whether to stay for the next one.

## Heat and ground

Heat waves can ignite exposed living entities that are neither spectators nor fire-immune. Open sky matters to those checks. Hot Crust appears on eligible natural ground and cools over time. Do not assume that the colour of ordinary terrain still tells you whether it is safe to stand there.

Running near existing soul flames can set off a visible chain of soul-fire effects and draw nearby monsters' attention. A soul torch or lantern is therefore worth noticing before you sprint past it. The effect does not require the storm to invent a new creature.

The default storm creature list contains blazes and magma cubes. Nether structure-fragment candidates include fortresses, bastion remnants and ruined Nether portals; the structure system still obeys its own limits and protection checks.

## Tempering equipment

Tempering happens to a **dropped, damageable item** when a heat wave passes it in the storm's heart, within half the storm's radius. Wearing an item or standing near the column is not the same trigger.

In Minecraft 1.21.1, tempering increases maximum durability by **50%**. Armour gains **+1 armour toughness**; an eligible item with an attack-damage attribute gains **+1 attack damage** in the main hand. The item receives a tempered marker, lore and a glint. An already tempered item is not tempered repeatedly.

Modded equipment can behave differently. Try an ordinary tool first before risking something you cannot replace.

Tempering is an opportunity inside a dangerous area, not a promise that everything dropped into fire will survive. Recovery is still your problem.
