# A Deep Dark storm hears a voice (Simple Voice Chat's decoded frames, made up here): loud speech inside it is heard, quiet breath is not, nothing is heard outside.
/storm clear
/time set 6000
/gamemode creative
/spreadplayers 0 0 0 300 false @s
wait 60
/tp @s ~ ~ ~ 0 0
wait 20
/storm spawn deep_dark 120
wait 900
# outside the storm: not heard
voice loud
wait 10
pulses
# into the column (its wall 80 blocks ahead, its centre 140)
/tp @s ~ ~ ~140
wait 40
voice quiet
wait 10
pulses
voice loud
wait 10
pulses
shot voice_heard
